How We Work

The principles behind every deal.

Our team is small on purpose. It keeps us close to the work, accountable to our partners, and honest about what we build. These are the values everyone here is measured against.

Discipline over hype

We would rather pass on a deal than force one. Conservative underwriting and secured positions come before upside, on every project we take on.

Integrity in every deal

Clear terms, honest updates, and paperwork that protects our partners. What we say is what we do, in writing, on time.

Community first

We invest in neighborhoods, not just numbers. Every renovation aims to leave a place better than we found it and stronger for the people who live there.

Craftsmanship

Thoughtful, high quality renovations that create homes people are proud of and buyers want. We treat every property like it carries our name.

Leadership

Meet the people building Purpose Driven REI.

Ricardo Carrillo

Founder & CEO

Ricardo brings 20+ years of real-world experience in real estate, actively buying, renovating, and reselling residential properties across Southern California and beyond. With more than 200 successful fix-and-flip projects closed and a growing footprint in new construction, he has seen the questions come up on every deal.

His approach is simple: source the right properties, structure every deal conservatively, protect investor capital with real security, and treat every project as a chance to leave a neighborhood better than he found it. That discipline is the standard he holds the whole team to.

  • 20+ years of investing & sales experience in SoCal
  • 200+ successful fix & flip projects closed
  • Active in deal sourcing, private money & new construction
  • Founder of a community built on purpose
