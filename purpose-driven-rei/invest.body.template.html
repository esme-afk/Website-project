Investor FAQ

Answers before you ask.

What is the minimum investment?
You can get started with a minimum of $10,000. From there, the right amount depends on the project and your goals. The best next step is a short call so we can match you to a fitting opportunity.
How exactly is my capital secured?
Every investment is backed by a signed promissory note and a deed of trust recorded against the property. You hold a real, documented position in a physical asset, in a first or second lien position agreed in advance.
How long is my money invested?
Terms typically run 6 to 12 months, tied to the scope of the renovation and the market. Your specific term is defined in your note before you fund.
How and when am I paid?
You receive your principal plus your agreed return at the completion of the project. Many partners choose to reinvest into the next opportunity to keep their capital working.
What happens if a project runs long?
Real estate timelines can shift. Our conservative loan-to-value targets and secured position are designed to protect your principal even when a project takes longer than planned. We communicate early and often if a timeline changes.
Are returns guaranteed?
No. Real estate investments carry risk, including market shifts, economic conditions, and unforeseen events. Figures on this page are illustrative examples of typical structures, not a promise of performance. We focus on securing your position and managing risk conservatively.
