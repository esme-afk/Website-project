The Opportunity

Great real estate deals are won with speed and capital. As a private investor, you supply the capital and we do the work: sourcing, structuring, renovating, and selling. You earn a defined return, secured by the property itself, without swinging a hammer or managing a single contractor.

How It Works

A disciplined process, from wire to return.

No guesswork and no surprises. Every investment follows the same clear, documented path, so you always know where your capital is and how it is protected.

01

Fund a specific deal

You review and fund a specific, vetted project. No blind pools. You know the property, the numbers, and the plan before a dollar moves.

02

Secured in writing

Your investment is documented with a promissory note and secured by a deed of trust recorded against the property, in first or second position.

03

We execute the plan

Our team acquires and renovates the property using a proven, in-house process refined over 200 plus projects and two decades in the market.

04

The property exits

The finished home sells or refinances. Our conservative loan-to-value targets are built to protect your position even if the market shifts.

05

You are repaid

You receive your principal plus your agreed return, typically within 6 to 12 months, and you are first in line to fund the next one.

Investor FAQ

The questions investors ask first.

What is the minimum investment?
Minimums vary by project and are set during your investor call. We will walk you through current opportunities and find the position that fits your capital and goals.
How is my capital secured?
Every investment is documented with a promissory note and secured by a deed of trust recorded against the specific property, in a defined first or second lien position, with conservative loan-to-value.
What kind of returns can I expect?
Typical structures target a 10 to 15% return over a 6 to 12 month term, depending on lien position and project. These are illustrative examples, not guarantees. We will review real, current numbers together.
How long is my capital committed?
Most projects run 6 to 12 months. You will know the expected timeline for a specific deal before you fund it, and you are first in line to redeploy into the next project.
What happens if a project underperforms?
Your position is protected by a recorded deed of trust and a conservative loan-to-value that leaves an equity cushion. Real estate carries risk and nothing is guaranteed, but the structure is built to protect your principal first.
Can I invest through a retirement account?
Many investors use self-directed retirement accounts to fund private real estate. We can point you to the right custodians and walk your team through the process.
How do I get started?
Book an investor call or send us your details below. We will share our full Private Money Investment Overview and current opportunities, then structure a position that fits you.
