Our Story

Built to profit with purpose.

Purpose Driven REI was founded on a simple belief: real estate should build lasting wealth for the people who invest in it, and lift the communities it touches along the way.

Driven By A Shared Mission To

Revitalize Properties. Maximize Returns. Elevate Communities.

We acquire, renovate, and resell underperforming residential properties into high performing assets, delivering strong, secured returns for our investment partners while improving neighborhoods and strengthening the communities we invest in.

What started as one investor's hands-on approach to fix and flip has grown into a full network of partners, operators, and private lenders who build value together, one deal at a time.

Our Founder

Meet Ricardo Carrillo.

Ricardo Carrillo

Founder & CEO

Ricardo brings 20+ years of real-world experience in real estate, actively buying, renovating, and reselling residential properties across Southern California and beyond. With more than 200 successful fix-and-flip projects closed and a growing footprint in new construction, he has seen the questions come up on every deal, and built a network designed to answer them.

His approach is simple: source the right properties, structure every deal conservatively, protect investor capital with real security, and treat every project as a chance to leave a neighborhood better than he found it.

  • 20+ years of investing & sales experience in SoCal
  • 200+ successful fix & flip projects closed
  • Active in deal sourcing, private money & new construction
  • Founder of a community built on purpose

Our Approach

How we build value, on every deal.

Discipline over hype. Every project runs through the same playbook, so our partners always know how their capital is working and how it is protected.

01

Source

We find underperforming properties with real upside, in markets we know, at prices that leave room for a conservative return.

02

Transform

Thoughtful, high quality renovation turns tired homes into desirable assets that buyers want and neighborhoods are proud of.

03

Protect

Investor capital is secured by real estate, with a promissory note and recorded deed of trust on every deal.

04

Return

We deliver strong, secured returns to our partners and reinvest into the next project, and the next community.
