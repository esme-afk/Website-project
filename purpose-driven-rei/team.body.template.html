How We Work

The principles behind every deal.

Our team is small on purpose. It keeps us close to the work, accountable to our partners, and honest about what we build. These are the values everyone here is measured against.

Discipline over hype

We would rather pass on a deal than force one. Conservative underwriting and secured positions come before upside, on every project we take on.

Integrity in every deal

Clear terms, honest updates, and paperwork that protects our partners. What we say is what we do, in writing, on time.

Community first

We invest in neighborhoods, not just numbers. Every renovation aims to leave a place better than we found it and stronger for the people who live there.

Craftsmanship

Thoughtful, high quality renovations that create homes people are proud of and buyers want. We treat every property like it carries our name.

Founder & CEO

Ricardo Carrillo

Founder & CEO

Ricardo founded Purpose Driven REI on a simple belief: real estate should build real wealth and improve the neighborhoods it touches. For 20+ years he has personally sourced, financed, renovated, and sold residential property across Southern California and beyond.

With more than 200 fix-and-flip projects closed and a growing new-construction pipeline, he has learned exactly where deals go wrong and how to protect the people funding them. That hard-won experience is now the playbook the entire team runs on.

"I treat every investor's capital the way I treat my own. Secured first, deployed with discipline, and returned on time."
