Purpose Driven REI is more than deals. It is a growing community of investors, agents, and partners who learn together, network together, and win together, led by someone who shows up and does the work in public.

Led With Purpose

Built on relationships, mentorship, and real value.

Ricardo Carrillo believes the fastest way to grow in real estate is to grow with other people. So he built a community around it: regular Zooms that bring real value to investors, agents, and partners, live meetups on real properties, and networking events where genuine relationships get made.

He leads as a mentor first. Structure, integrity, and generosity run through everything, because when the people around you win, everybody wins. That is what it means to profit with purpose.

Ways To Connect

Four ways to plug in and grow.

Whether you learn best on a screen, on a job site, or over a handshake, there is a place for you in this community.

Value-Packed Zooms

Live virtual sessions where Ricardo breaks down real deals, answers questions, and brings actionable value to investors, agents, and partners.

Live Property Meetups

On-site meetups at active projects where you see exactly how a real fix and flip is analyzed, negotiated, and executed, in person.

Networking Events

In-person events to build genuine relationships with investors, agents, lenders, and operators who are actively closing deals.

Mentorship & Leadership

Ricardo leads with a mentor's heart, helping the next generation of investors grow with structure, discipline, and purpose.
