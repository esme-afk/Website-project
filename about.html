Story Behind Lone Wolf

Lone Wolf Hauling Inc. was built by Salvador Mendez with a simple standard: treat every project like the customer is trusting us with more than just debris. Whether it is a home cleanout, a jobsite, or a business cleanup, people need clear answers, reliable timing, and someone who takes the details seriously.

With over decades in business, Lone Wolf is family-owned, owner-led, and built around trust.

The name Lone Wolf represents strength, independence, instinct, and protection. For us, that means showing up with the right equipment, helping customers choose the right dumpster, and making the rental process feel easier from the first call. We know most people do not rent dumpsters every day, so we explain what matters before the container lands on the property. That is the kind of service we want customers to remember.

Locally owned roll-off and dumpster rental serving San Diego County since 2010.
