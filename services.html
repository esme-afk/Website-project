The Right Size Depends on What You Are Loading

A heavy load needs a different setup than a bulky cleanup. Roof tile, dirt, concrete, asphalt, green waste, and trash all behave differently once they are inside the container.

At Lone Wolf Hauling Inc., we look at what you are loading, where the dumpster is going, and how long you need it before recommending a roll-off. That helps you avoid paying for the wrong size, choosing the wrong container, or running into issues once the job is already moving.

10YD

10-Yard Low Boy

Used for dense, heavy materials like dirt, concrete, asphalt, sod, grass, and weeds.

25YD

25-Yard Dumpster

Used for roof tile, trash, cleanouts, furniture removal, and medium-size cleanup projects.

40-Yard Dumpster

A Clear Process Before the Dumpster Arrives

We keep the rental process simple, but we do not skip the important details. A few questions up front can prevent the wrong dumpster, the wrong placement, or the wrong material category.

  1. 1

    Call or Text Your Project Details

    Send your city, project type, material, delivery timeline, and a photo if you have one.

  2. 2

    We Match the Dumpster to the Load

    We look at material, weight, volume, rental time, and placement so we can recommend the right option.

  3. 3

    We Confirm Delivery Details

    We review access, placement, schedule, and any details that may affect the drop-off.

  4. 4

    You Load Approved Materials

    You fill the dumpster during your rental period with the material that was quoted and approved.

  5. 5

    We Pick It Up

    When the rental is complete, we return for pickup and haul the load away.

Dumpster Care

Our Dumpsters Represent Our Name Before We Even Say a Word

We care about the condition of our dumpsters. They are built for heavy work, but they should still arrive looking professional, ready to load, and appropriate for the property they are placed on.

A clean, well-maintained dumpster gives customers more confidence, protects the appearance of the site, and shows that the company takes the rental seriously.

Maintained for Real Work

Our roll-offs are used for dirt, concrete, asphalt, roof tile, trash, green waste, construction debris, and demolition cleanup. We keep them ready for the kind of work our customers actually need.

Presented With the Property in Mind

A dumpster at a home, business, or jobsite should be placed with care. We pay attention to access, space, and the way the container sits on the property.

Checked Between Rentals

Before a dumpster goes back out, we pay attention to its condition so the next customer receives equipment that is ready for the job.

Matched to the Project

Low boys, 25-yard dumpsters, and 40-yard dumpsters all serve different purposes. We help make sure the container fits the work instead of forcing every project into the same option.
