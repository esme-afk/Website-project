Choose the Right Dumpster for Your Project

The right dumpster size depends on weight, material type, loading space, and rental time — all affect what makes sense for your project. Tell us what you are throwing away and we will help you match the project to the right dumpster size.

Match the Dumpster to the Load

A small load of concrete can weigh more than a large load of household junk. Green waste, roof tile, clean dirt, asphalt, and trash all fill and weigh differently. The right dumpster is the one that fits the material, the weight, the space on your property, and how long you need it.

Use this guide as a starting point. If you are unsure, call or text us before booking and we will help you choose the right option.

10YD

10-Yard Low Boy

Best for heavy material loads

Our 10-yard low boy is built lower to the ground, which makes it easier to load dense materials like clean dirt, concrete, asphalt, sod, grass, and weeds.

This is usually the better option when the material is heavy and you need a container that is easier to access without overloading the truck.
